WEDNESDAY – WEB SERIES KNOWLEDGE BASE
1. General Information

Wednesday is an American supernatural mystery comedy television series based on the character Wednesday Addams from The Addams Family. The series was developed by Alfred Gough and Miles Millar.

The show premiered on Netflix on November 23, 2022. It combines elements of dark comedy, fantasy, horror, mystery, and coming-of-age drama.

The main character is Wednesday Addams, a highly intelligent, sarcastic, emotionless-looking teenager who has a fascination with death, mysteries, and the supernatural.

The series follows Wednesday's experiences at Nevermore Academy, a school for students with supernatural abilities and unusual characteristics.

2. Main Character – Wednesday Addams

Wednesday Addams is the central character of the series. She is the daughter of Morticia and Gomez Addams.

Wednesday is intelligent, independent, observant, sarcastic, and highly skilled at solving mysteries. She dislikes social conventions and prefers spending time alone.

She has psychic abilities that allow her to experience visions when touching people or objects. These visions help her investigate mysterious events.

Wednesday is portrayed by Jenna Ortega.

Her personality is characterized by:

Dark humor
Intelligence and analytical thinking
Emotional independence
Interest in crime and mysteries
Strong determination
Excellent investigative skills
3. Nevermore Academy

Nevermore Academy is a fictional boarding school attended by Wednesday Addams.

The academy is designed for outcasts, including students with supernatural abilities and unusual identities.

Some types of students at Nevermore include:

Werewolves
Sirens
Vampires
Gorgons
Psychics

Nevermore Academy plays a central role in the story because many of the mysteries and supernatural events take place around the school.

The academy also provides the setting for Wednesday's friendships, rivalries, investigations, and personal development.

4. Important Characters
Wednesday Addams

The protagonist of the series. She investigates supernatural mysteries and strange crimes while attending Nevermore Academy.

Enid Sinclair

Enid is Wednesday's roommate and one of her closest friends. Unlike Wednesday, Enid is cheerful, colorful, friendly, and expressive. She is a werewolf who initially struggles with transforming into her wolf form.

Tyler Galpin

Tyler is a local teenager who works at a coffee shop in the town of Jericho. He initially appears friendly toward Wednesday and becomes romantically interested in her. Later, he is revealed to have a significant connection to the Hyde mystery.

Xavier Thorpe

Xavier is a student at Nevermore Academy who possesses artistic psychic abilities. His drawings can come to life. He develops complicated feelings toward Wednesday.

Bianca Barclay

Bianca is a popular student at Nevermore Academy and a talented siren. She initially has a rivalry with Wednesday but later becomes involved in several important events.

Morticia Addams
	
Morticia is Wednesday's mother. She is elegant, mysterious, and has a complicated history connected to Nevermore Academy.

Gomez Addams

Gomez is Wednesday's father. He is loving, humorous, and deeply devoted to his family.

Eugene Ottinger

Eugene is a student at Nevermore who loves beekeeping. He becomes one of Wednesday's allies during her investigation.

Thing

Thing is a living, disembodied hand that helps Wednesday. It acts as her assistant, companion, and messenger.

5. Season 1 Storyline

The first season begins when Wednesday Addams is expelled from her previous school after an incident involving piranhas and students who bullied her brother, Pugsley.

Her parents send her to Nevermore Academy, where they previously studied.

At Nevermore, Wednesday struggles to fit in and initially dislikes the school environment. However, she becomes involved in investigating a series of mysterious murders occurring near the town of Jericho.

Wednesday discovers that she has psychic visions and begins uncovering secrets related to Nevermore Academy, her family history, and an ancient mystery involving Joseph Crackstone.

The investigation eventually reveals that Tyler Galpin is the Hyde monster responsible for the attacks.

The season ends with Wednesday stopping the threat and leaving Nevermore with several unanswered questions about her future.

6. Important Locations
Nevermore Academy

The main school where Wednesday studies. It is a boarding school for supernatural outcasts.

Jericho

A nearby town located close to Nevermore Academy. It has a historical connection to the academy and plays an important role in the mystery.

The Addams Family Home

The family residence of Wednesday Addams, where she lives with her parents and brother before attending Nevermore.

The Weathervane

A coffee shop in Jericho where Tyler works. Wednesday frequently visits the location.

7. Supernatural Elements

The Wednesday series includes several supernatural abilities and creatures.

Psychic Visions

Wednesday experiences visions of past or future events through physical contact with people or objects.

Werewolves

Werewolves are students who can transform into wolves. Enid Sinclair is one of the prominent werewolf characters.

Sirens

Sirens possess the ability to influence people through their voices. Bianca Barclay is a siren.

Hydes

A Hyde is a dangerous supernatural creature with a monstrous form. Tyler Galpin is revealed to be a Hyde.

Gorgons

Gorgons are supernatural beings associated with snakes and the ability to turn people into stone. Ajax Petropolus is a Gorgon.

8. Themes of the Series

The Wednesday series explores several themes:

Identity and self-discovery: Wednesday learns more about herself and her supernatural abilities.
Friendship: Her relationship with Enid shows the importance of friendship despite personality differences.
Family history: Wednesday investigates secrets connected to her parents and ancestors.
Acceptance: Nevermore Academy represents a place where unusual individuals can belong.
Mystery and investigation: The story revolves around uncovering hidden truths.
Individuality: Wednesday values independence and refuses to follow social expectations.

Wednesday Season 2 continues Wednesday Addams's story at Nevermore Academy.

12. Summary

Wednesday is a supernatural mystery television series centered on Wednesday Addams, a teenage girl who attends Nevermore Academy. While learning to understand her psychic abilities, Wednesday investigates mysterious murders and uncovers secrets involving the school, her family, and the supernatural world.

The series combines mystery, dark humor, fantasy, horror, friendship, and coming-of-age storytelling. Its major characters include Wednesday Addams, Enid Sinclair, Tyler Galpin, Xavier Thorpe, Bianca Barclay, and Thing.

Human Resource (HR) Policies – With Numeric Details
1. Working Hours Policy: Employees must work 8 hours per day, from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, with a 1-hour lunch break.

2. Attendance Policy: Employees must maintain a minimum of 95% monthly attendance.

3. Late Coming Policy: Employees are allowed a maximum of 3 late arrivals per month. After that, salary deductions or leave adjustments may apply as per company rules.

4. Leave Policy: Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves, 12 sick leaves, and 18 earned leaves annually.

5. Work From Home Policy: Employees can avail up to 2 work-from-home days per month with prior approval.

6. Probation Policy: Newly joined employees will have a probation period of 6 months.

7. Notice Period Policy: Employees must serve a 30-day notice period before resignation.

8. Performance Review Policy: Employee performance will be evaluated twice a year (every 6 months).

9. Training Policy: Every employee must complete at least 12 hours of professional training annually.

10. Overtime Policy: Employees working beyond 9 hours per day may be eligible for overtime benefits as per company policy and applicable law.

11. Employee Grievance Policy: HR will acknowledge employee complaints within 2 working days and aim to resolve them within 7 working days.

12. Dress Code Policy: Employees must follow the prescribed formal or business-casual dress code for at least 5 working days, where applicable.

13. Confidentiality Policy: Employees must not disclose confidential company information during employment or after leaving the organization.

14. Holiday Policy: The organization will provide at least 10–12 declared holidays annually, subject to applicable laws.

15. Exit Policy: Employees must complete all exit formalities and hand over company assets within 5 working days of their last working day.

Note: These are sample HR policy guidelines. Numeric limits should be customized according to company requirements and applicable Indian labour laws.

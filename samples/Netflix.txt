History and Development of Netflix
Netflix is one of the world's most popular online entertainment and streaming platforms. It was founded on August 29, 1997, by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, California, USA.

1. 1997 – Foundation: Netflix was established as a company that aimed to make renting movies easier and more convenient for customers.

2. 1998 – Website Launch: Netflix launched its official website, allowing customers to order DVDs online.

3. 1999 – Subscription Service: Netflix introduced a monthly subscription plan that allowed customers to rent DVDs without worrying about individual late fees.

4. 2000 – Recommendation System: Netflix introduced a personalized movie recommendation system to help customers discover movies based on their interests.

5. 2002 – Public Company: Netflix became a publicly traded company on the NASDAQ stock exchange.

6. 2007 – Online Streaming: Netflix introduced its streaming service, allowing subscribers to watch movies and television shows directly over the internet.

7. 2010 – International Expansion: Netflix expanded its services to Canada, marking the beginning of its international growth.

8. 2013 – Original Content: Netflix started producing its own original television series. House of Cards became one of its first major original productions.

9. 2015 – First Original Film: Netflix released its first original feature film, Beasts of No Nation.

10. 2016 – Global Expansion: Netflix expanded to more than 190 countries, making its entertainment services available worldwide, including India.

11. 2017 – Major Achievement: Netflix reached approximately 100 million subscribers around the world.

12. 2018 – Growing Popularity: Netflix continued expanding its original content, including popular series, films, and documentaries.

13. 2020 – Growth During COVID-19: During the COVID-19 pandemic, Netflix gained millions of new subscribers as people spent more time at home.

14. 2021 – Gaming Expansion: Netflix entered the mobile gaming industry and began offering games to its subscribers.

15. 2022 – New Subscription Plans: Netflix introduced an advertising-supported subscription plan in some countries.

16. 2023 – Password Sharing Changes: Netflix introduced new rules to limit account sharing outside a household.

17. 2024–2026 – Continued Development: Netflix continued investing in international films, web series, live entertainment, gaming, and new technologies to improve the viewing experience.

Conclusion
Netflix has transformed the entertainment industry through technology, innovation, and original content. It started as a small DVD rental business and developed into a global streaming platform. Today, millions of people use Netflix to watch movies, television shows, documentaries, and web series in different languages. It has changed how people access and enjoy entertainment around the world.
